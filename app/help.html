For students

How do I join a class?

Open Find a batch, filter by subject, area and monthly fee, then open the batch you want and pay to take your seat. Your seat is confirmed straight away.

What is a batch?

A batch is a group class that runs on fixed days at a fixed time, and every student in it pays one monthly fee. It is how coaching already works in Bangladesh, so nothing about it should feel new.

Where are my classes?

Under My classes. Each batch has a class room with the live class and a chat with your tutor and classmates.

Can I rate my tutor?

Yes, once you have joined a batch. Only students who actually paid can leave a review, which is why the ratings mean something.

For teachers

How do I start teaching?

Create an account as a tutor, fill in your profile and add a certificate. Our team checks every tutor before their batches can go live.

How do batches work?

You open a batch with its own days, time, seat limit and monthly fee. One tutor can run several batches at different fees — a Class 9 batch and an HSC batch do not have to cost the same.

How do I run a live class?

Open the class room for a batch and choose how to run it: a group class hosted in the page, a direct browser-to-browser call for a few students, or your own Zoom or Meet link. Pressing start notifies every student in that batch.

When do I get paid?

The moment a student joins. You keep 85% of the fee and HelloStudents keeps 15%. Every single payment is itemised on your dashboard.

Payments

Payments are in demo mode. No real money moves yet. The wallet, the payment record and the tutor’s earnings are all real, but the card and mobile-wallet step is simulated until the payment gateway is switched on.

Two ways to pay

Pay for one batch directly, or top up your wallet once and join batches from the balance.

Is my money safe?

Every payment is recorded and shown in your wallet history. Your browser is never allowed to mark a payment as complete — only the payment provider can — so a seat can never be taken without a real payment behind it.

Refunds

If a class does not happen, contact us and we will return the fee to your wallet.

About HelloStudents

Most private tuition in Bangladesh runs through coaching centres that find the students, set the fee and keep most of the money. The teacher carries the reputation and receives a fraction.

HelloStudents removes the centre from the middle. A teacher sets their own days, their own seats and their own monthly fee, and students find them by subject, area and price.

The platform is newly launched. We would rather show you small honest numbers than invented ones.

Privacy

What we store

Your name, phone number and email, the batches you joined, your payments, and messages you send in a class chat.

Who can see it

Your tutor sees your name and phone number once you join their batch. Other students see only your name in the class chat. Your wallet and payment history are visible to you alone.

Live classes

Your name is shown over the video during a live class. This is deliberate: if a recording is ever leaked, it can be traced back. Every time someone opens a class room, that is recorded too.

What we never do

We do not sell your details, we do not pass your phone number to advertisers, and we do not use class recordings to train software.

Terms

For everyone

One account per person. Do not share your login. Treat tutors and classmates with respect in class chats.

For students

A fee covers one month in one batch. Recording or re-sharing a class is not allowed and can end your account.

For tutors

Teach the batch you advertised, at the days and times you published. HelloStudents keeps 15% of each fee. Repeatedly missing your own classes can suspend your account.

This is a young platform

Features are still being added and some, such as card payments, are clearly marked as not finished. We will always tell you which parts are real rather than pretend everything works.
