Part 1

Wireframes

These fix information hierarchy and control placement. No colour, type or spacing decision is implied — the single accent hue below marks only the primary action or the one element a screen turns on.

Screens are ordered by the marketplace loop: a student discovers a tutor, books, pays, attends, and rates; the tutor and admin surfaces that make that loop work follow. Labels are drawn in English for legibility; production copy is Bangla-first, and Bangla appears here wherever it changes the layout — the language toggle, Bengali numerals, and the Sunday-first week.

1.3Search results — student

  • Repeat rate is surfaced on the card, in the accent. It is behavioural rather than stated, making it the most honest quality signal available, and it is the metric the whole business is judged on.
  • Position 3 is an exploration slot. A share of page-one positions is reserved for randomised new tutors. Without it, ranking is self-reinforcing — no bookings means no reviews means no ranking — and the supply funnel ossifies.
  • No scarcity theatre. No view counts, no "3 people are looking", no countdowns. In a market where parents are already wary of being taken advantage of, manufactured urgency reads as a scam.

1.4Tutor profile — public, server-rendered

Marketplace constraint

No phone number, email, or address appears anywhere on this page, and none is returned by the API that renders it. Contact detail is exchanged only after a confirmed booking. Having removed the coaching centre from between tutor and student, the platform becomes the next thing that can be removed — leakage is the largest existential risk to the model, and contact masking is the cheapest structural defence.

Part 2

Data schema

PostgreSQL 16, 41 tables across 12 modules. The schema is drawn per module rather than as one unreadable diagram, because module ownership is a real enforced constraint: a module may reach another module's tables only through its service interface, never by a SQL join.

Conventions: primary keys are prefixed ULIDs (ses_01HQ…) — sortable, safe to expose, self-identifying in logs. Money is a BIGINT count of poisha (1 BDT = 100 poisha); no floats, anywhere. Times are timestamptz in UTC, rendered in Asia/Dhaka. Phone numbers are stored E.164 and are the primary identifier.

Why the taxonomy is modelled, not typed

Four curricula coexist in Bangladesh, and a student in one will not accept results from another. NCTB English version is not English medium — same national syllabus and same board exams, taught in English — and conflating the two is an expensive, common modelling error. Grade levels are stored as codes rather than integers because an integer cannot represent "A Level Year 1", and SSC/O Level equivalence is a search-expansion concern, not an identity. Subjects are curated with hand-written Bangla, English and transliterated aliases, because students search "phy 1st paper" and "পদার্থ" in roughly equal measure.

Three layers, not two. An offering is what is sold and for how much; a session is a specific instant that occupies the tutor's calendar; a booking is one student's paid claim on a seat. Collapsing offering into session — the obvious shortcut — makes recurring classes, price history and cancellation policy impossible to express. Collapsing session into booking makes group classes impossible.

2.5Booking conflicts — the mechanism

Why a constraint and not an application check

The naive version — SELECT … WHERE ranges overlap, then INSERT — is a race condition with a comment on it: two requests both read "no conflict" before either writes, and the evening booking peak is exactly when that window is widest. An advisory lock works only if every future call site remembers it, and some future admin tool, bulk importer or migration script will not.

A database constraint cannot be forgotten, cannot be raced, and survives every code path the team has not written yet. The same shape is applied to bookings on student_id, which is what produces the blocked 8:00 PM slot in screen 1.5. occupies is written by the application rather than generated, because timestamptz ± interval is only STABLE and an exclusion constraint requires IMMUTABLE inputs.

A correction to the original brief

The brief specified "buy N hours, deduct per class". Taken literally — one credit equals one hour, spendable with any tutor — that breaks the moment two tutors charge different rates. A student buys 10 hours at an implied ৳500/hr, then books a ৳900/hr tutor: either the platform absorbs ৳400 a session, or the "hour" was never an hour. No pricing of a fungible hour-credit works in a marketplace where sellers set their own prices — it only works where a central authority sets one price, which is the model this product exists to compete against.

The schema above resolves it with two instruments. Wallet credits are money-pegged (1 credit = 1 poisha) and fungible, presented to users as taka and expressed in hours wherever a tutor context exists — "৳2,400, about 2.6 hours with Rifat". Hour packs are the literal "buy N hours" product, scoped to one tutor so the rate is unambiguous, at a locked price. The brief's intent is fully preserved and the arithmetic now works — and hour packs are commercially stronger, because they lock a rate, commit the student to one tutor (which lifts repeat rate, the metric that matters most), and give the tutor guaranteed forward income.

erDiagram

        bigint gross_poisha
        bigint commission_poisha
        numeric commission_rate "snapshot, never retroactive"
        bigint net_poisha "CHECK gross = commission plus net"
        text status "accruing payable batched paid reversed held"
        timestamptz payable_at "session end plus 48h dispute window"
    }
    PAYOUTS {
        text id PK
        text tutor_id FK
        text payout_method_id FK
        bigint amount_poisha
        text status "pending sent confirmed failed"
        text provider_ref
        text idempotency_key UK "never double pay"
    }
    TUTOR_PAYOUT_METHODS {
        text id PK
        text tutor_id FK
        enum method "bkash nagad bank"
        bytea account_ref_enc "encrypted MSISDN or account number"
        text account_last4 "display only"
        text account_name "matched against verified identity"
        timestamptz verified_at
    }
  • CHECK (gross = commission + net) turns a rounding bug into a write-time failure, instead of a few poisha of drift per session that compounds into an unexplainable variance months later.
  • Changing a payout method needs OTP step-up and a 24-hour cooling period, during which payouts are held and the previous number is notified. Payout-detail substitution is the single highest-value attack on this system, and this is what blocks it: an attacker who takes over an account still cannot extract money before the real owner is told.
  • Commission rate is stored per earnings row, so a future rate change never rewrites history and a tutor can always be shown exactly what was deducted and why.

reviews.booking_id UNIQUE is the entire review-integrity model compressed into one constraint. Manufacturing a fake review requires manufacturing a paid, attended booking — which costs real money and leaves an immutable ledger trail. Nearly every review-fraud problem other marketplaces suffer is a consequence of not doing this.

2.10Database-enforced invariants

What the schema guarantees without any application code cooperating. Each of these would otherwise live in a service method, be forgotten by some future call path, and fail only under production concurrency.

ConstraintTableGuarantees
EXCLUDE gist (tutor_id, occupies)class_sessionsA tutor is never in two sessions at once, under any concurrency
EXCLUDE gist (student_id, occupies)bookingsA student is never in two classes at once
CHECK seats_taken <= capacityclass_sessionsA group session is never oversold
UNIQUE (session_id, student_id)bookingsOne seat per student per session
CHECK balance_credits >= 0credit_accountsA wallet can never go negative
UNIQUE (idempotency_key)ledger_entriesA retried payment never double-credits
UNIQUE (idempotency_key)payoutsA retried batch never double-pays a tutor
CHECK gross = commission + netearningsCommission arithmetic can never silently drift
UNIQUE (booking_id)reviewsNo paid, attended booking means no review
UNIQUE (booking_id)earningsExactly one earnings row per booking
UNIQUE (provider, provider_ref)paymentsOne payment per provider reference
UNIQUE (source, external_id)webhook_eventsA replayed webhook is processed exactly once
UNIQUE (session_id)recordingsOne recording per session
UNIQUE (phone_e164)usersOne human, one account
Verified nightly

A reconciliation job re-asserts each invariant by query — including the constraint-enforced ones, in case a migration accidentally dropped one — and any mismatch pages the on-call engineer with no threshold. A ledger that does not balance means either a bug losing money or a bug inventing it, and both are urgent.
